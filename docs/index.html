A help desk for Gmail and Outlook

The help desk that lives in your inbox.

Verlass turns the side panel of your email into a help desk. People send a request and follow it without leaving their inbox. Your team sees its queue, assigns work, replies, and keeps notes in the same place. It suits an office of five, a school, a nonprofit, or a company with many sites.

Try the live demo Install Verlass

Your data stays yours. Tickets live in a Google Sheet in your own Google account, or in a list in your own Microsoft 365. Verlass has no server, no account to create, and no tracking.

What it does

Requests from the inbox

Anyone in the organization opens Verlass beside their mail, describes what they need, and gets a ticket number and email updates. They can reply, close, or reopen their own requests.

Queues for the team

The team works from queues for open, unassigned, assigned to me, overdue, waiting, finished, and all tickets. It can search by number, name, or words, and filter by category, priority, or person.

Assignment rules

Send printer problems to one person and password resets to another, by category, priority, location, or a keyword. Anything else can take turns around the team.

Due times that respect working hours

Each priority has a time allowed. Only your working days and hours count, with your days off skipped, and overdue tickets rise to the top.

Replies and internal notes

Reply to the requester, or leave a note only the team can read. Every change is written to an activity log.

Email in your words

There are seven notices, each with wording you can change and placeholders for the ticket's details. Switches turn them off.

Made to be changed

Categories, priorities, statuses, locations, the team, the word for a ticket, the number format, colors, and logo are all settings. Nobody edits code.

Create a ticket from an email

When someone emails the team instead, open the message and turn it into a ticket in their name.

Where it runs

Gmail and Google Workspace

A Google Workspace add-on. Tickets and settings are rows in one Google Sheet. The Private install keeps that Sheet closed to everyone but the help desk, and asks staff for the fewest permissions.

Install for Gmail

Outlook and Microsoft 365

An Outlook add-in for the web, Windows, Mac, and mobile. Tickets are items in a SharePoint list in your own tenant, and each person sees only their own requests.

Free for everyone

Verlass is free and open source under the MIT License, for personal, commercial, school, and enterprise use. Read the source code, change it, and run your own copy if you like.

Version 2.0.0 is new. It has been tested against simulated Gmail, Google Sheets, and Microsoft 365 services and in a real browser, and has not yet been run in a live Google account or a live Microsoft 365 tenant. Try it with a few people first, and please report what you find.

Verlass is the German word for dependability. "Auf uns ist Verlass" means "you can count on us."
