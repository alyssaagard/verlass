Try the whole help desk

This is the real Verlass panel with sample tickets for an invented organization. It runs entirely in this browser tab: there is no sign-in, and nothing you type is sent anywhere.

Who are you?

Things to try

  • As Morgan, send a new request. Then open the menu (the three dots) and choose Email outbox to read the notices a real help desk would have sent.
  • As Sam, open the Overdue or Unassigned queue, assign a ticket to yourself, and reply.
  • As Alex, open Settings. Rename a category, add a priority, write an assignment rule, or change the wording of an email and watch the preview.
  • Switch between Dark, Light, and System at the top of the page or in the menu.

Changes last until you close this tab. Choose Reset the demo in the menu to start again.
